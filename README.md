## Overview

This group project explores how artificial intelligence (AI) is changing the software developer job market. As AI tools become more common in software development, they may influence hiring trends, required skills, daily tasks, and opportunities for junior developers.

Our goal is to examine these changes using research and evidence while distinguishing between documented findings and our own interpretations.

## Research Questions

- How is AI affecting software developer hiring trends?
- How is AI changing developers' daily tasks?
- What challenges and opportunities does AI create for junior developers?
- What skills should students develop to prepare for the changing job market?

## Key Considerations

- Changes in work tasks do not automatically mean that software developer jobs are disappearing.
- Attitudes toward AI-generated code are not the same as actual AI error rates.
- Claims about hiring trends should be supported by relevant recruitment data.
- Our analysis distinguishes research findings from illustrative examples and our own interpretations.

## Project Goal

Our goal is to understand how AI may reshape the software developer job market and identify the skills students need to prepare for the future of software development.

This project considers both the challenges and opportunities associated with AI rather than assuming that AI will only replace developers.
