# Jihyun Park - How AI Is Changing the Software Developer Job Market

Target speaking time: approximately 25 seconds.

## Script

Have you ever wondered how AI is changing the way software developers in job market?

AI is changing the skills companies need and how developers work. According to Indeed Hiring Lab's 2025 report, AI is changing how tasks are performed and increasing the importance of human judgment.

Today, we will explore hiring trends, changing developer tasks, and opportunities for future developers.

## Source

Indeed Hiring Lab — AI at Work Report (2025)

https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2025/09/23/ai-at-work-report-2025-how-genai-is-rewiring-the-dna-of-jobs/

## Notes

- The introduction presents the main question of our project.
- The Indeed report provides background on how AI is changing work.
- The report discusses changes in work tasks; it does not by itself prove that AI is reducing software developer jobs.
- The presentation will explore hiring trends, changes in daily tasks, and opportunities for junior developers.
