# Atrina Ranjbaran — Hiring Trends

**Target speaking time:** approximately 25 seconds.

## Script

Software hiring is recovering, but the recovery is uneven. HackerRank's 2025 report found stronger hiring activity for lead and senior developers than for junior and entry-level developers. LinkedIn's U.S. report says core programming skills remain important, while Python, cloud, and AI-related skills are gaining ground. These reports show changing employer needs, but they do not prove AI caused weaker entry-level hiring.

## Sources

- HackerRank — [2025 Developer Skills Report](https://embed.hackerrank.com/reports/developer-skills-report-2025)
- LinkedIn Economic Graph — [U.S. Software Engineer Talent Landscape (February 2026)](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/us-software-engineer-talent-landscape-2026.pdf)

## Notes

- HackerRank's percentages describe hiring activity signals; they are not counts of all job openings.
- LinkedIn's report describes U.S. hiring trends.
- The reports do not prove that AI caused weaker entry-level hiring.
