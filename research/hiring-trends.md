# Part B: Hiring Trends — Atrina

## Verified facts
- HackerRank's 2025 report found hiring activity rose year over year by 22% for lead developers, 19% for senior developers, 9% for junior developers, and 7% for entry-level hiring. These are hiring activity signals, not counts of all job openings.
- LinkedIn's February 2026 U.S. report says core programming remains important, while Python, cloud, and AI-related skills are gaining ground.
- LinkedIn reports growth in AI Engineer hiring, but says its findings do not prove AI caused weaker entry-level hiring.
- Indeed Hiring Lab's 2025 AI at Work report gives background on how generative AI changes work tasks. It is not direct evidence that software developer hiring is rising or falling.

## Speaking notes
Hiring trends are mixed. HackerRank found stronger hiring activity for senior and lead developers than for junior and entry-level developers. LinkedIn reports that core programming remains important, while Python, cloud, and AI-related skills are gaining ground. The reports show changing employer needs, but they do not prove that AI caused weaker entry-level hiring.
