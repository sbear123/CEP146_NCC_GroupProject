# How AI Changes Developers' Daily Tasks

## Purpose 
Explain how AI assistance changes daily development tasks and what this means for a developer's responsibilities.

## Research Evidence
Stack Overflow's 2025 Developer Survey reports that 46% of respondents distrusted the accuracy of AI tool outputs, compared with 33% who trusted them.
Source: [https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai]
Section: Accuracy of AI tools
Accessed: October 17, 2026

## Our Analysis
AI can assist with drafting code and suggesting tests. Developers still need to understand requirements, review the code, debug problems, and verify the results.

Our interpretation is that generating code is only one part of a developers' work. Evaluating its correctness remains an important responsibility.

## Illustrative Example
AI may draft a login feature. A developer then checks whether incorrect passwords are rejected saftely.

This is a hypothetical example, not an actual experiment.

## Evidence Limitations
-The survey reports respondents' attitudes toward AI accuracy.
-The 46% figure is not an AI error rate.
-These findings do not directly measure hiring requirements.
-They do not prove that AI causes developer job losses.
