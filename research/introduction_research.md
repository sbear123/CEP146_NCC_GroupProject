# How AI Is Changing the Software Developer Job Market

## Purpose

Introduce the main question of our project and explain why AI's impact on the software developer job market is important. Present the Indeed Hiring Lab report as background for exploring hiring trends, changing skill requirements, and opportunities for junior developers.

## Research Evidence

Indeed Hiring Lab's 2025 AI at Work Report examines how generative AI is changing the way work is performed and how it affects job tasks and responsibilities.

Source: [https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2025/09/23/ai-at-work-report-2025-how-genai-is-rewiring-the-dna-of-jobs/]

Report: AI at Work Report (2025)

## Our Analysis

AI is changing how people perform some work tasks, including tasks that involve software development. These changes may affect the skills employers look for and the responsibilities of software developers.

Our project will explore how AI relates to software hiring trends, demand for AI skills, and opportunities for junior developers.

However, changes in work tasks do not automatically mean that software developer jobs are disappearing.

## Illustrative Example

A company may look for a junior developer who can write code, solve problems, and use AI tools responsibly.

This is a hypothetical example, not an actual job posting.

## Evidence Limitations

- The report discusses the impact of generative AI on work and job tasks.
- Changes in work tasks do not necessarily indicate an increase or decrease in software developer hiring.
- The report should not be treated as direct proof that AI is eliminating junior developer jobs.
- Specific claims about software hiring trends and demand for AI skills require additional evidence from relevant hiring data.
